OUR STORY

Clean Clothes. Fresh Homes.

High Tee Classics is a liquid laundry soap brand focused on providing a reliable cleaning solution for everyday homes.

Beyond laundry, the product can also be used for household cleaning including floors, bathrooms and other surfaces.
